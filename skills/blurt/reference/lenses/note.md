# lens: note — an observation, finding or piece of knowledge worth keeping

Use for: research while browsing ("这家的定价是按席位的"), how something works, competitor facts, learnings,
decisions made out loud, anything that isn't a fix, an idea or a todo.

Fields:
- `summary` — the point in 1–3 sentences.
- `details` — markdown, optional: specifics, numbers, quotes from the screen.
- `tags`; `source.url` when visible.
